## Tool
Mindtrip

## Prompt
Help me plan a holiday of 2 weeks. I want to explore a magical place, filled with wonder and Christmas sparkle. Make sure I've got lots of things to discover.

---

I'd love to explore Rovaniemi. Is there enough to do to fill 2 weeks? I don't mind renting a car and exploring.

## Result
Rovaniemi, Lapland – 14-Day Magical Christmas Itinerary 🎄✨
An immersive holiday of wonder, hidden gems, and Lappish flavors for two enchanting weeks

Day 1 – ✨ Arrival & Arctic Welcome
Settle in and embrace the snowy magic of Lapland
☀️ Morning: Arrive in Rovaniemi → Pick up your rental car → Short orientation drive
🌤️ Afternoon: Explore central Rovaniemi on foot → Warm up with coffee at a local café → Stroll along the riverside
🌙 Evening: Check in to SnowHotel & Glass Igloos → Enjoy your first Finnish sauna

Day 2 – 🎅 Santa’s Village & Arctic Circle
Meet Santa and step into the heart of Christmas
☀️ Morning: Visit Santa Claus Village → Meet Santa, send a postcard from the Arctic Circle
🌤️ Afternoon: Try snowmobiling or a husky ride around the village → Browse Lapland handicraft shops
🌙 Evening: Dinner in the village → Optional: Northern Lights tour

Day 3 – 🏛️ Arktikum & Local Culture
Discover Arctic history and Sámi heritage
☀️ Morning: Explore Arktikum Science Museum
🌤️ Afternoon: Stroll by the Ounasjoki River → Visit the Rovaniemi Church
🌙 Evening: Sample traditional Lappish cuisine in town

Day 4 – 🐺 Husky & Reindeer Adventure
Classic Lapland experiences in snowy forests
☀️ Morning: Husky safari (local provider)
🌤️ Afternoon: Visit a reindeer farm for a sleigh ride and Sámi culture
🌙 Evening: Sauna at your hotel

Day 5 – 🧊 Arctic SnowHotel & Ice Experiences
Explore wintry beauty and ice art
☀️ Morning: Tour SnowHotel & Glass Igloos: ice suites, ice bar, snow chapel
🌤️ Afternoon: Try ice fishing or a snow sculpting workshop
🌙 Evening: Dine at the Ice Restaurant → Watch for Northern Lights from your glass igloo

Day 6 – 🦉 Wildlife Wonders at Ranua
See Arctic animals on a day trip
☀️ Morning: Drive to Ranua Wildlife Park
🌤️ Afternoon: Explore the park, see polar bears → Lunch at the café
🌙 Evening: Return to Rovaniemi → Relaxing sauna

Day 7 – ❄️ Winter Sports & Free Time
Try Finnish winter fun or unwind
☀️ Morning: Cross-country skiing or snowshoeing nearby
🌤️ Afternoon: Spa visit, local shopping, or guided art walk
🌙 Evening: Cozy dinner in the city

Day 8 – 🎨 Art, Culture & Cozy Cafés
Discover local creativity and warm Lapland hospitality
☀️ Morning: Explore Culture House Korundi → Local art and events
🌤️ Afternoon: Coffee and pastries at Hostel Café Koti
🌙 Evening: Dinner at Roka Street Bistro

Day 9 – 🌲 Arboretum Adventure & Local Markets
Nature and Lapland’s seasonal flavors
☀️ Morning: Explore Arktikum Arboretum
🌤️ Afternoon: Visit Rovaniemi Market for local cheeses, berries, and pastries
🌙 Evening: Dinner at Nili Restaurant

Day 10 – 🦌 Real Reindeer Encounters
Connect with Lapland’s most magical animals
☀️ Morning: Visit Arctic Reindeer Oy for a personal reindeer experience
🌤️ Afternoon: Rustic Finnish coffee and short reindeer sleigh ride
🌙 Evening: Dinner at Lapland Restaurant Kotahovi

Day 11 – 🧑‍🍳 Local Salmon & Arctic Cuisine
Eat like a local, from fire-cooked salmon to fine dining
☀️ Morning: Lunch at Santa's Salmon Place
🌤️ Afternoon: River walk, visit bakeries for Lappish pastries
🌙 Evening: Tasting menu at Sky Kitchen & View

Day 12 – ❄️ Free Day: Your Own Winter Adventure
Relax, revisit favorites, or try something new
☀️ Morning: Optional: Revisit Arktikum Science Museum or try snowshoeing
🌤️ Afternoon: Coffee break, last souvenir shopping
🌙 Evening: Light dinner or hot chocolate at Hostel Café Koti

Day 13 – 🎁 Final Discoveries & Christmas Magic
Wrap up your adventure with your favorite moments
☀️ Morning: Return to a favorite spot—art, market, or a snowy walk
🌤️ Afternoon: Visit the Christmas House at Santa Claus Village
🌙 Evening: Farewell dinner at your favorite restaurant

Day 14 – ✈️ Farewell Lapland
Say goodbye to your winter wonderland
☀️ Morning: Breakfast at your hotel → Final stroll in the city or by the river
🌤️ Afternoon: Return your rental car, last souvenirs
🌙 Evening: Depart from Rovaniemi or relax if staying one more night
